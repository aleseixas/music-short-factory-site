Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

This Policy describes information that may be processed when a creator uses Music Short Factory or authorizes a supported platform integration.

1. Information we may process

  • basic account identifiers and display information returned by an authorized platform;
  • authorization tokens and technical credentials needed to perform requested integration actions;
  • videos, captions, titles, hashtags, thumbnails, and publishing settings;
  • technical logs used for security, error diagnosis, status checking, and reliability.

2. How information is used

  • to authenticate authorized creator accounts;
  • to prepare and perform supported publishing operations;
  • to check publication status and report errors;
  • to protect and maintain the service.

3. TikTok data

If TikTok integration is enabled, TikTok account data and authorization credentials are used only to provide enabled TikTok functionality for the authorized account. Music Short Factory does not sell TikTok user data.

4. Service providers

Information may be transmitted to service providers only as needed to operate a requested feature, such as temporarily hosting media or sending content to an authorized social platform. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

5. Temporary media

Some workflows may temporarily upload rendered media to infrastructure providers so an authorized platform can retrieve it. The workflow may delete temporary media after processing, subject to provider caching, backup, and retention behavior.

6. Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the enabled service, maintain security and reliability, or satisfy legal obligations. Retention may vary based on the connected platform and deployment configuration.

7. Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect credentials and user information. Production secrets should be stored using restricted environment or secret-management controls rather than public source code.

8. User choices and deletion

Users may stop using the service and revoke platform authorization through the applicable platform. Requests concerning information controlled by the Music Short Factory operator can be sent to the contact address below.

9. Children's privacy

Music Short Factory is not directed to children and is not intended to knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law.

10. Changes

This Policy may be updated as the service or legal requirements change. The current version and effective date will remain published at this URL.
