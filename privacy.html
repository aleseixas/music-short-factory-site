Privacy Policy

This Policy explains what information Além do Hit processes through its public pages, the Music Short Factory creator tool, support communications, and its authorized TikTok publishing integration.

Effective August 28, 2026Last updated August 30, 2026

At a glance. Além do Hit uses data only to operate the creator-requested workflow described below. TikTok tokens are encrypted on the server, uploaded videos are temporary, and no video is transferred without explicit confirmation. Personal information and TikTok user data are not sold.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Além do Hit public pages, support communications, the Music Short Factory web workflow, its minimum secure backend, and the TikTok connection operated for the creator tool. Além do Hit is the primary public brand; Music Short Factory is the creation and publishing workflow.

Third-party services, including TikTok and the providers used to host the public pages and backend, process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

2. Information we process

The information processed depends on which parts of the project or integration a creator chooses to use.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • provide and protect the creator's essential session;
  • support a browser-local preview and, only after explicit confirmation, receive, validate, and temporarily stage the video selected by the creator;
  • identify and authenticate a platform account authorized by the creator;
  • carry out a TikTok draft upload or Direct Post requested and explicitly confirmed by the creator;
  • display the authorized account, load account-specific publishing options and restrictions from TikTok, and check publishing status;
  • provide support and respond to privacy or deletion requests;
  • maintain security, diagnose errors, prevent misuse, and improve reliability; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce the Terms of Service.

4. TikTok integration

The creator tool uses TikTok Login Kit to connect an account chosen by the creator. It requests user.info.basic to identify and display that account, video.upload to send a creator-confirmed video as a draft, and video.publish to load creator-specific publishing options and perform a creator-confirmed Direct Post.

For Direct Post, the backend queries TikTok's creator information before publishing. The tool shows only the privacy options and comments, Duet, Stitch, duration, and other restrictions TikTok returns for the authorized account. The creator previews the video, edits the caption, chooses from those available settings, completes any required content and music disclosures, and explicitly confirms before the video is sent.

For a draft upload, the creator completes the caption and final settings inside TikTok. For Direct Post, the creator makes that decision in the tool immediately before selecting Publish directly. Além do Hit does not send a draft or publish directly without the creator's action and confirmation.

TikTok client credentials and OAuth tokens are never placed in the public frontend. Access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest on the backend.

5. Essential cookie

The creator tool uses one strictly necessary, opaque session cookie to maintain the authorization session, protect state-changing requests, and associate the creator with the encrypted TikTok connection and upload status held on the backend. It is not used for advertising, behavioral analytics, or cross-site tracking.

The cookie is configured with appropriate security attributes in production, including HTTPS-only transport. Blocking or deleting it may end the active session or prevent the creator workflow from functioning.

6. When information may be shared

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary with:

  • TikTok. TikTok receives the creator-confirmed video, authorization information, selected publishing settings, required disclosures, and transfer details needed to create a draft or Direct Post and report its status.
  • Necessary providers. Limited information may be handled by providers supplying HTTPS hosting, temporary media transfer, storage, security, or diagnostic infrastructure.
  • Legal and safety recipients. Information may be preserved or disclosed when required by applicable law, valid legal process, security needs, or the protection of rights and safety.

Além do Hit does not sell personal information or TikTok user data and does not share it for behavioral advertising.

7. Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to maintain the creator's authorized connection, complete a confirmed draft or Direct Post transfer, check status, secure the operation, respond to support, or meet a legal obligation.

The selected video is stored only in a temporary upload location and is normally deleted immediately after the transfer attempt completes or fails. If a process interruption leaves a temporary file behind, startup and scheduled cleanup remove recognized upload files after they are more than six hours old. Videos are not kept as a media library.

Essential sessions are configured for no more than 30 days and use a seven-day default. Expired sessions and their associated records are deleted when cleanup runs. Disconnecting removes the encrypted authorization credentials and upload-status records while leaving an anonymous essential session; Delete my data removes the session and other associated application records. A verified deletion request provides another route, subject only to limited records that may need to be retained for security or legal reasons.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. These measures include HTTPS in production, OAuth state validation, an essential opaque session cookie, anti-forgery protection for state-changing actions, encrypted token storage, file-type and size validation, temporary upload deletion, and restricted access to operational data.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe an authorized connection may be at risk, remove the connection through the platform and contact support.

9. Public pages and creator tool

The public informational pages are served as static files and do not themselves store TikTok credentials. The creator-tool interface communicates with a separate secure backend over HTTPS for TikTok authorization, protected session handling, temporary video transfer, upload status, disconnection, and data deletion.

Hosting providers may process standard request information, such as IP address, browser or device information, and request timestamps, under their own policies. Your email provider processes any message you choose to send through an email link. Além do Hit does not intentionally use advertising cookies or advertising trackers.

10. Revocation, deletion, and other choices

You may decline authorization, use Disconnect TikTok in the creator tool, or remove the connection through TikTok's connected-app settings. Disconnecting through the tool ends the application connection and deletes the stored authorization credentials associated with the session. Removing access in TikTok prevents future authorized requests but may not automatically delete other information already controlled by the operator.

To request access, correction, or deletion of operator-controlled personal information, email alelseixas@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or tokens. Reasonable verification may be requested to protect the account and data involved.

See Revocation & Data Deletion for step-by-step instructions. Limited information may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or documenting completion of a request.

11. Children's privacy

Além do Hit is not directed to children and is not intended to knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. Contact the operator if you believe such information has been provided.

12. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated as the project, integrations, or applicable requirements change. The current version and update date will remain available at this URL.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, security concerns, revocation help, or data deletion, contact Além do Hit at alelseixas@gmail.com.
