Privacy Policy

This Policy explains what information Além do Hit may process through its public website, support communications, internal Music Short Factory workflow, and authorized platform integrations.

At a glance. Além do Hit uses data only for the purposes described below. Personal information and TikTok user data are not sold. The public website has no account system, upload form, advertising tracker, or live publishing interface.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Além do Hit website, support communications, the internal Music Short Factory production workflow, and supported platform integrations operated for the project. Music Short Factory is a working name for the internal desktop workflow; Além do Hit is the primary public brand.

Third-party platforms, including TikTok and GitHub, process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

2. Information we may process

The information processed depends on which parts of the project or integration a creator chooses to use.

CategoryExamples
Support informationYour email address and the information you include in a support, privacy, or deletion request.
Creator materialsResearch notes, scripts, selected assets, narration, captions, images, videos, and related production files chosen for the workflow.
Authorized TikTok account informationTikTok account identifiers and limited basic profile information returned for an account that the creator authorizes.
Authorization informationOAuth authorization credentials, permission grants, connection state, and expiration information needed for an authorized integration.
Publishing content and choicesVideos, titles, captions, hashtags, destination account, privacy choices, and supported interaction settings selected by the creator.
Operational informationPublishing identifiers and status, timestamps, application events, diagnostic messages, security events, and technical logs needed to operate and troubleshoot a requested action.

Do not send passwords, authorization codes, tokens, client secrets, or other unnecessary credentials in a support message.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • organize research, scripts, production assets, videos, and metadata selected by the creator;
  • identify and authenticate a platform account authorized by the creator;
  • prepare or carry out a content upload or publication requested and confirmed by the creator;
  • display relevant account and publishing choices and check processing status;
  • provide support and respond to privacy or deletion requests;
  • maintain security, diagnose errors, prevent misuse, and improve reliability; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce the Terms of Service.

4. TikTok integration

Além do Hit is being integrated with the TikTok Content Posting API. If the integration is enabled and a creator chooses to use it, TikTok's authorization process is used to connect a creator-selected account. The application may then process limited basic account information, OAuth authorization credentials, the video and caption prepared by the creator, available publishing settings, and status or diagnostic information necessary for the creator-requested operation.

Content must not be sent to a TikTok account that has not authorized access. The intended Direct Post experience keeps the authorized destination, content preview, editable caption or title, available privacy and interaction choices, and final confirmation visible to the creator.

Confidential credentials are not placed in this public website. This static GitHub Pages site does not perform TikTok login, token exchange, uploads, or publishing.

5. When information may be shared

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary with:

  • Connected platforms. TikTok or another creator-selected destination receives content, metadata, settings, and authorization details needed for a requested operation.
  • Necessary providers. Limited information may be handled by providers supplying hosting, media transfer, storage, security, or diagnostic infrastructure.
  • Legal and safety recipients. Information may be preserved or disclosed when required by applicable law, valid legal process, security needs, or the protection of rights and safety.

Além do Hit does not sell personal information or TikTok user data and does not share it for behavioral advertising.

6. Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, such as maintaining an authorized connection, completing a creator-requested transfer, checking status, securing the operation, responding to support, or meeting a legal obligation.

Authorization information is removed or invalidated when it is no longer needed, access is revoked, or a verified deletion request is completed, subject to limited records that may be retained for security or legal reasons. Temporary media used for a transfer is scheduled for deletion or allowed to expire after the operational period reasonably needed to complete or troubleshoot it.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. Access to operational data should be limited, and confidential platform credentials must remain outside the public website and public source code.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe an authorized connection may be at risk, remove the connection through the platform and contact support.

8. Public website

This website is a static GitHub Pages site. It does not create user accounts, accept file uploads, store TikTok credentials, or publish content. It does not intentionally use advertising cookies or advertising trackers.

GitHub may process standard hosting information, such as IP address, browser or device information, and request timestamps, under its own policies. Your email provider processes any message you choose to send through an email link.

9. Revocation, deletion, and other choices

You may decline to connect an account or remove an existing TikTok connection through TikTok's connected-app settings. Revoking access stops future authorized requests, but it may not automatically delete information already controlled by the operator or remove content already published on TikTok.

To request access, correction, or deletion of operator-controlled personal information, email alelseixas@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or tokens. Reasonable verification may be requested to protect the account and data involved.

See Revocation & Data Deletion for step-by-step instructions. Limited information may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or documenting completion of a request.

10. Children's privacy

Além do Hit is not directed to children and is not intended to knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. Contact the operator if you believe such information has been provided.

11. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated as the project, integrations, or applicable requirements change. The current version and update date will remain available at this URL.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, security concerns, revocation help, or data deletion, contact Além do Hit at alelseixas@gmail.com.
