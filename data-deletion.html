Revocation & Data Deletion

Disconnecting the creator tool, removing access in TikTok, and deleting data held by Além do Hit are related but distinct actions. You can complete any or all of them.

Remove TikTok accessRequest data deletionPrivacy Policy

Action 1

Disconnect or revoke TikTok access.

Revocation prevents future requests using that authorization. It does not remove a draft or post already held by TikTok, and revocation inside TikTok may not by itself erase other information controlled by the operator.

From the creator tool

  1. 1

    Open the Além do Hit creator tool in the browser used for your session.

  2. 2

    Select Disconnect TikTok and confirm.

  3. 3

    The backend ends the connection and deletes the OAuth credentials stored for that session.

From TikTok

  1. 1

    Open TikTok and tap Profile.

  2. 2

    Tap the Menu button, then Settings and privacy.

  3. 3

    Open Security & permissions, then Apps and services permissions.

  4. 4

    Select the application associated with Além do Hit. The name shown should match the name presented during authorization.

  5. 5

    Tap Remove access and confirm.

What revocation changes

Know what remains under each party's control.

01

Future access

Disconnecting or removing access prevents future authorized draft-upload requests for that TikTok account.

02

Operator-held data

The creator-tool disconnect deletes stored OAuth credentials. Use the separate deletion action below for other data associated with the session.

03

TikTok-hosted drafts

Manage or delete a transferred draft or published post through your own TikTok account controls.

Action 2

Delete operator-controlled data.

If your creator-tool session is active, use Delete my data for the most direct route. You can also submit an email request. Reasonable verification may be required to protect your data.

In the creator tool

Open the creator tool, select Delete my data, and confirm. The backend deletes the session's stored TikTok connection, account data, upload status, and related operator-controlled records. The essential session cookie is then cleared.

Use the subject Além do Hit Data Deletion Request and include:

  • the email address or other information used with the workflow;
  • your TikTok display name or account identifier, if relevant and known;
  • whether the request covers all data or specific records; and
  • any non-sensitive context needed to locate the records.
Do not send secrets. Never include your TikTok password, authorization code, access token, refresh token, client secret, or another credential.

Request scope

What may be reviewed for deletion.

A deletion action or verified request may cover the essential session record, TikTok account identifiers, basic authorized profile information, encrypted OAuth authorization credentials, upload status information, and related technical records controlled by the operator. Selected videos are normally deleted immediately after the transfer attempt; interrupted-process remnants are removed by cleanup after they are more than six hours old.

Important distinction. Além do Hit can act only on data under the operator's control. TikTok controls information and content hosted on TikTok. Limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or documenting completion of the request.
