Revocation & Data Deletion

Removing TikTok access and deleting data held by the Além do Hit operator are separate actions. You may choose either one or both.

Remove TikTok accessRequest data deletionPrivacy Policy

Action 1

Remove the application's TikTok access.

Revocation prevents future requests using that authorization. It does not automatically remove an existing TikTok post or delete information already controlled by the operator.

  1. 1

    Open TikTok and tap Profile.

  2. 2

    Tap the Menu button, then Settings and privacy.

  3. 3

    Open Security & permissions, then Apps and services permissions.

  4. 4

    Select the application associated with Além do Hit. The name shown should match the name presented during authorization.

  5. 5

    Tap Remove access and confirm.

What revocation changes

Know what remains under each party's control.

01

Future access

Removing access prevents the integration from making future authorized requests for that TikTok account.

02

Operator-held data

Revocation may not erase records already controlled by the operator. Use the separate request below for deletion.

03

TikTok-hosted posts

Manage or delete content already published on TikTok through your own TikTok account controls.

Action 2

Request deletion of operator-controlled data.

Send enough non-sensitive information to identify the relevant records. Reasonable verification may be requested to protect your data.

Data deletion email alelseixas@gmail.com

Use the subject Além do Hit Data Deletion Request and include:

  • the email address or other information used with the workflow;
  • your TikTok display name or account identifier, if relevant and known;
  • whether the request covers all data or specific records; and
  • any non-sensitive context needed to locate the records.
Do not send secrets. Never include your TikTok password, authorization code, access token, refresh token, client secret, or another credential.
Start an email request

Request scope

What may be reviewed for deletion.

A verified request may cover TikTok account identifiers, basic authorized profile information, OAuth authorization credentials, videos and publishing metadata, settings, status information, and technical logs controlled by the operator.

Important distinction. Além do Hit can act only on data under the operator's control. TikTok controls information and content hosted on TikTok. Limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or documenting completion of the request.
