About the creator tool

A focused workflow for short-form music storytelling.

Além do Hit gives creators a deliberate path for reviewing a finished, research-led music story and sending it as a draft to a TikTok account they authorize.

PurposeHow it worksMusic Short FactoryCreator control

Purpose

A controlled path from a finished music story to a TikTok draft.

The tool is designed for concise educational and documentary stories about songs, artists, recording processes, production choices, and music culture.

It keeps the workflow narrow: prepare the story and video, connect a creator-owned TikTok account, preview the selected file, explicitly confirm the upload, and finish the draft inside TikTok.

Audience

For creators with a story of their own to tell.

Além do Hit is intended for independent creators producing short-form music education, commentary, or documentary content.

How it works

Five steps from preparation to a TikTok draft.

The workflow uses TikTok's Upload API. It does not Direct Post: the creator completes the final editing and publishing decision inside TikTok.

  1. 01

    Prepare

    Create the short-form music video.

    Before using the upload screen, the creator assembles research, a script, selected media, narration, captions, and editing into the finished vertical video they intend to send.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Authorize your own TikTok account.

    TikTok Login Kit presents the requested user.info.basic and video.upload permissions. The creator decides whether to authorize the connection.

  3. 03

    Preview

    Review the exact video before transfer.

    The creator tool shows the selected file and connected destination. The creator can stop or select a different video before confirming.

  4. 04

    Confirm upload

    Approve one specific draft transfer.

    The tool requires an explicit confirmation. Only then does the backend use video.upload to send the video to the authorized account as a draft.

  5. 05

    Finish in TikTok

    Choose post settings and decide whether to publish.

    The creator opens the draft from TikTok's inbox or editor, reviews or adds the caption, chooses the available privacy and interaction settings, and makes the final publication decision there.

Creation technology

Music Short Factory is the workflow behind the tool.

Music Short Factory is the name of the broader editorial path covering research, scripts, assets, narration, captions, finished-video review, and TikTok draft delivery.

Focused by design. The current web tool does not create or edit the video. It provides only creator authorization, local preview, explicit confirmation, draft upload, and status checking. Final post settings and publication remain in TikTok.

TikTok integration

Draft upload to an authorized account.

The creator tool connects through TikTok Login Kit and uses only user.info.basic and video.upload. Tokens are handled by the secure backend, never exposed in the public frontend. A transfer starts only after the creator previews the selected video and explicitly confirms.

The result is a draft, not an automatic post. TikTok's own inbox or editor presents the caption, privacy, comments, and other settings available to that account before the creator chooses whether to publish.
