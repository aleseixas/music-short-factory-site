About the project

Music deserves more than a passing mention.

Além do Hit is an independent editorial project focused on the stories, decisions, people, and cultural context behind music.

PurposeHow it worksInternal workflowPrinciples

Purpose

Explain the music, not just the moment.

The project researches and produces concise educational and documentary stories about songs, artists, recording processes, production choices, and music culture.

The goal is to make useful context accessible in a short-form format while preserving careful research, editorial review, and a clear point of view.

Audience

For curious listeners and music storytellers.

Além do Hit is intended for people who want more context than a clip, headline, or isolated fact can provide.

01

Listeners

People interested in the meaning, history, and creative decisions behind the music they hear.

02

Students and educators

People exploring songwriting, recording, arrangement, production techniques, and cultural context.

03

Creators

Music storytellers looking for a structured example of research-led short-form production.

How it works

Five editorial stages, one coherent story.

The process connects research, writing, production, review, and publishing preparation without removing human judgment.

  1. 01

    Research

    Find the question and verify the context.

    Sources, notes, dates, credits, recording details, and possible angles are gathered and organized. The story starts with a focused question rather than a generic topic.

  2. 02

    Story preparation

    Turn research into a concise narrative.

    The angle becomes a script, narration plan, and scene structure. Claims are reviewed, unnecessary detail is removed, and the central idea stays clear.

  3. 03

    Video production

    Build the vertical story.

    Creator-selected images and other assets are assembled with narration, captions, pacing, and editing for a short-form format.

  4. 04

    Review

    Check the story before it leaves the desk.

    The script, factual context, visuals, captions, audio, final render, and publishing metadata can be reviewed and revised.

  5. 05

    Publishing

    Prepare the destination and confirm the action.

    The creator chooses an authorized destination, checks the available settings, reviews the content and caption, and explicitly confirms a supported publishing action.

Internal technology

Music Short Factory supports this production process.

Music Short Factory is the internal desktop workflow used by Além do Hit to organize research, scripts, assets, narration, captions, video production, review, metadata, and publishing preparation.

Clear boundary. It is described here as part of how the editorial project works, not as a large public SaaS platform. This website does not offer accounts, a dashboard, a public installer, or live publishing controls.

Editorial principles

Useful technology, accountable decisions.

The creator remains responsible for the story and the final publication decision.

  • Research firstSources and context shape the story.
  • Human reviewScripts and finished videos are checked before publication.
  • Rights awarenessCreators remain responsible for permissions and appropriate media use.
  • Creator controlMetadata, destination, settings, and confirmation stay visible.

TikTok integration

In development for authorized publishing.

Além do Hit is being integrated with the TikTok Content Posting API to support creator-requested video delivery to authorized TikTok accounts. Authorization is provided through TikTok, and the intended publishing flow requires content review and explicit creator confirmation.

OAuth credentials and publishing operations do not belong in this static website. No secret or token is placed in the public frontend.
