FAQ

Common support questions.

What is Além do Hit?

Além do Hit is a creator tool and editorial project for producing and delivering short-form educational and documentary music stories.

What is Music Short Factory?

It is the name of the broader editorial workflow within Além do Hit. The current web tool handles account connection, local preview, creator confirmation, TikTok draft delivery, and status checking; creators prepare the finished video before selecting it.

What TikTok access does the tool request?

The tool requests user.info.basic to display the account the creator authorizes and video.upload to transfer a creator-confirmed video as a draft. It does not request video.publish.

Does Além do Hit publish without authorization?

No. A creator must authorize the destination account and explicitly confirm each transfer. The result is a draft; final settings and publication are completed inside TikTok.

Where do I choose caption, privacy, comments, and other post settings?

After a successful draft transfer, open TikTok's inbox or editor. TikTok presents the options available for your account there. Além do Hit does not select them on your behalf.

What should I do if an upload does not appear?

Keep the upload status shown by the creator tool, check TikTok's inbox and notifications, and contact support with the time of the attempt and any non-sensitive status message. Never send passwords or tokens.

How can I revoke TikTok access?

Use TikTok's connected-app controls to review the application and remove access. The Data Deletion page provides the current menu path and explains what revocation does.

How can I request deletion of my data?

Email alelseixas@gmail.com with the subject “Além do Hit Data Deletion Request.” See the request instructions before sending.

Is Além do Hit affiliated with TikTok?

No. Além do Hit is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok.
