FAQ

Common support questions.

What is Além do Hit?

Além do Hit is an independent editorial project producing short-form educational and documentary stories about songs, artists, production, and music culture.

What is Music Short Factory?

It is the internal workflow used to organize research, scripts, assets, video production, review, metadata, and publishing preparation for Além do Hit.

What TikTok access may the integration use?

The integration being developed may use basic authorized account information and the permissions needed for creator-requested content delivery. The authorization screen presented by TikTok identifies the access requested before a creator chooses whether to continue.

Does Além do Hit publish without authorization?

No. Content must not be sent to a TikTok account that has not authorized the integration, and the intended publishing flow requires explicit creator confirmation.

How can I revoke TikTok access?

Use TikTok's connected-app controls to review the application and remove access. The Data Deletion page provides the current menu path and explains what revocation does.

How can I request deletion of my data?

Email alelseixas@gmail.com with the subject “Além do Hit Data Deletion Request.” See the request instructions before sending.

Is Além do Hit affiliated with TikTok?

No. Além do Hit is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok.
