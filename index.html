Creator control

Nothing is uploaded without a deliberate action.

  • You connect your own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit.
  • You see the connected account and preview the selected video.
  • You explicitly confirm before the video is transferred.
  • For a draft, you finish settings in TikTok. For Direct Post, you choose only from settings TikTok returns for your account.

TikTok Content Posting API

Choose draft or creator-confirmed Direct Post.

The creator tool uses TikTok Login Kit with user.info.basic to identify the account a creator authorizes. It uses video.upload for draft delivery and video.publish for Direct Post, only after the creator selects a video, reviews its preview, and explicitly confirms the action.

For Direct Post, the tool first queries TikTok's creator_info endpoint and presents only the caption, privacy, comments, Duet, Stitch, duration, and disclosure controls available for that authorized account. For a draft, the creator completes the final settings inside TikTok.

FAQ

Useful answers, without the sales pitch.

What is Além do Hit?

Além do Hit is a creator tool and editorial project for producing short-form educational and documentary music stories.

What is Music Short Factory?

Music Short Factory is the name of the broader editorial workflow within Além do Hit. The current web tool implements account connection, local preview, creator confirmation, draft or Direct Post delivery, and status checking; it does not create or edit the video in the browser.

What TikTok permissions does the tool use?

It requests user.info.basic to show the authorized account, video.upload to send drafts, and video.publish to load Creator Info and perform an explicitly confirmed Direct Post.

Does Além do Hit publish directly?

Only when the creator selects Direct Post, reviews the video and TikTok-provided settings, completes the required confirmations, and selects Publish directly. Draft upload remains available.

Is Além do Hit affiliated with TikTok?

No. Além do Hit is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok.
