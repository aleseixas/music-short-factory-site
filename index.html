Creator tool

Short-form music stories, built from research to video.

Music Short Factory is a desktop-based creator workflow for researching music stories, structuring narration, rendering vertical videos, and preparing content for supported social publishing integrations.

About

Built for music storytelling

The project focuses on educational and documentary-style short videos about songs, artists, recording stories, production details, and music culture.

Research

Organize verified sources and identify a concise narrative angle.

Production

Combine narration, captions, real visual assets, motion, and transitions in a vertical format.

Publishing preparation

Prepare platform-specific titles, captions, and media for accounts authorized by the creator.

Workflow

How it works

1. Select a story

Start with a song, artist, recording story, or other music-history topic.

2. Research and structure

Collect sources, write a concise script, and define the visual timeline.

3. Render

Generate a mobile-first vertical video with narration, captions, images, and motion.

4. Prepare for publishing

Create the media and metadata needed by supported social-platform integrations.

Connected platforms

Authorized-account publishing

When a platform integration is enabled, Music Short Factory uses official developer interfaces and authorization credentials for the account selected by the creator. It does not publish to accounts that have not authorized the application.

Account authorization

Connected-platform access is granted through the platform's authorization process.

Creator content

The workflow handles videos and metadata produced for the creator's publishing process.

Limited-purpose access

Platform credentials are used only for the enabled integration and related status operations.
