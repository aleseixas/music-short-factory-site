What we do

Music storytelling built on context.

We turn research into clear, engaging vertical stories while keeping editorial review at the center of the process.

01

Research

Sources, historical context, recording details, and production notes are organized around a focused story.

02

Storytelling

Research becomes an accessible script for viewers who want to understand the music, not only hear the headline.

03

Short-form video

Narration, images, captions, and editing are assembled for concise educational and documentary-style videos.

Who it is for

For people curious about how music is made and remembered.

Além do Hit is created for listeners, music students, creators, and anyone interested in the stories behind songs, artists, recordings, and scenes.

Editorial control

Technology supports the work. People make the decisions.

  • The subject, sources, and narrative angle are selected deliberately.
  • Scripts, visuals, narration, captions, and final edits are reviewed.
  • Publishing metadata and destination settings remain under creator control.
  • No publishing action should begin without explicit confirmation.

Platform integration

A transparent connection to authorized accounts.

Além do Hit is being integrated with the TikTok Content Posting API to support creator-requested video delivery to TikTok accounts that have authorized access.

The intended flow keeps the authorized account, content preview, publishing choices, and final confirmation visible to the creator. This public website does not connect accounts or publish content.

FAQ

Useful answers, without the sales pitch.

What is Além do Hit?

Além do Hit is an independent editorial project that creates short-form educational and documentary stories about music.

What is Music Short Factory?

Music Short Factory is the internal workflow used by Além do Hit to organize research, scripts, assets, video production, review, and publishing preparation. It is not presented as a standalone public SaaS product.

Can I download an app from this website?

No public download is offered here. This website presents the project, its workflow, support information, and public policies.

Does the website post to TikTok?

No. This is a static informational website. The TikTok integration is being developed for authorized, creator-requested publishing and is not implemented in this site.

Is Além do Hit affiliated with TikTok?

No. Além do Hit is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok.
