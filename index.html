Creator control

Nothing is uploaded without a deliberate action.

  • You connect your own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit.
  • You see the connected account and preview the selected video.
  • You explicitly confirm before the video is transferred.
  • You choose caption, privacy, comments, and other available options in TikTok before publishing the draft.

TikTok draft upload

Connect, confirm, then finish inside TikTok.

The creator tool uses TikTok Login Kit with user.info.basic to identify the account a creator authorizes. The Content Posting API permission video.upload is used only after the creator selects a video, reviews its preview, and explicitly confirms the transfer.

The video is sent to that authorized account as a draft. The creator then opens TikTok's inbox or editor to add or revise the caption, select the privacy and interaction options TikTok makes available, and decide whether to publish. Além do Hit does not Direct Post and does not publish a draft automatically.

FAQ

Useful answers, without the sales pitch.

What is Além do Hit?

Além do Hit is a creator tool and editorial project for producing short-form educational and documentary music stories.

What is Music Short Factory?

Music Short Factory is the name of the broader editorial workflow within Além do Hit. The current web tool implements account connection, local preview, creator confirmation, draft delivery, and status checking; it does not create or edit the video in the browser.

What TikTok permissions does the tool use?

It requests user.info.basic to show the authorized account and video.upload to send a creator-confirmed video to that account as a draft.

Does Além do Hit publish directly?

No. The tool uploads a draft. Caption, privacy, interaction settings, and the final publication decision are completed by the creator inside TikTok.

Is Além do Hit affiliated with TikTok?

No. Além do Hit is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok.
